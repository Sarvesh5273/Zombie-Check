# Narration handoff

Use the copy in elevenlabs-script.txt. It contains spoken words plus supported emotion/pause directions, without video timestamps or scene instructions.

Model: Eleven v4 or Eleven v3 (both support square-bracket audio tags). Choose a clear conversational voice and generate a single narration file.

Delivery: interested opening, warm explanation, confident results, cautious interpretation of old job posts, clear close.

The clean copy measured 188 written words. At 150 written words per minute plus four seconds of pauses, the estimate is 79.2 seconds; numeric pronunciations and model delivery can vary. Actual audio will be aligned after it is supplied.

The recording shows an audit completed earlier; it does not pretend a new 500-row audit ran during capture. Intake is described as configuration, and the 90-day flag does not establish a fake vacancy.

Return the generated MP3 or WAV in chat, or provide its absolute local path. The final narration/video merge will run locally with FFmpeg, without HyperFrames or paid media generation.
