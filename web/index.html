THE METHOD

Five signals. One clear paper trail.

  1. 01

    Import your original list

    Upload a CSV and select its company and website columns. Every original cell, duplicate, and row order is retained.

  2. 02

    Read the live pages

    TinyFetch reads fresh pages. TinySearch finds missing identities. TinySet performs structured research in saved batches.

  3. 03

    Weigh the evidence

    Recent releases and verified operating signals support an alive verdict. Older activity may mean slowing down. A zombie verdict needs explicit closure, discontinuation, or parking evidence.

  4. 04

    Inspect, resume, and export

    Review the sources, dates, and uncertainty for each row. Paused audits retain their checkpoints. Export the original CSV with the new evidence columns.
